# Cutest Picture — "Snowball Hug" (v1)

A baby seal pup hugging a snowball.

One idea in one sentence: a round white baby seal, enormous glossy eyes
looking straight at the viewer, both flippers wrapped around a snowball,
a tiny pink starfish on its head, on a soft empty pale-blue background.

## Why this one

The top of this competition's board is a monoculture — kittens hugging
chicks. This entry uses the same winning grammar with a subject nobody in
the top 15 uses: huge star-sparkle eyes, one big round baby subject
filling the frame, a small story touch (the snowball hug + the starfish),
soft pastel empty background, clean 3D-render craft, and a subject that
still reads clearly at 300 px.

Round 1 tried four subjects (red panda, seal pup, penguin chick,
capybara); the seal pup's face was the strongest. Round 2 refined it:
the snowball-hug version beat the plain pup and the two-pups-cuddling
version, because it keeps the single biggest face and eyes while adding
the cuddle story. The snowball also echoes the pup's own round white
shape, so the whole picture reads as one soft round idea.
